# Visual refresh

Status: tokens and shared components. No approval, routing, or schema change.

## Palette

The signature palette is Kiln: warm ivory paper (`#f4efe6`), espresso ink (`#1c140f`), and a deep clay accent (`#7c2f22`). Dark mode is the same materials at night: umber paper and a light clay accent (`#f0b089`). It is not a blue or purple SaaS theme. Body ink on paper is above 7:1. Every text and background pair used for status stays at least 4.5:1. Status is never color alone: badges keep their words, and the approval rail is only a second cue.

An industry accent changes only the accent hue (pine, copper, rose, sage, and so on) from the workspace pack. The paper, ink, and type stay the same.

## Type, space, motion

The root size is 18px. A reading-size control in the profile menu and in Settings offers 18, 20, and 22. The choice is stored in this browser and applied before first paint. Small labels stay at least 16px. Controls stay at least 44px. The chat-first layout keeps its wide gaps. Motion is a short rise with no fade. `prefers-reduced-motion` still turns animation off.
