# Trademark: preliminary look at public records (not a clearance opinion)

By Claude on 2026-11-27, from web searches of public trademark listings (mostly Justia mirrors of USPTO data, which can lag). **This is not a search of the USPTO database and not legal advice.** A real clearance needs an attorney using USPTO TESS/TSDR plus common-law and state searches.

## Names in use
- Product: "Scout OS" (the repo calls the product Scout OS / Workforce OS). Company/brand: "Ascend Software".

## "SCOUT" is crowded in software and related fields
Public listings show many SCOUT marks in computer software / SaaS (classes 9 and 42), including:
- SCOUT, Serial 99821296: AI chatbot assistant software and SaaS for customer support and task automation (pending as listed). Close to what Scout OS does.
- SCOUT, Serial 99062068: SaaS for business analytics and brand-visibility tracking (pending as listed).
- ROOFSCOUT (Scout Technology, Inc., Serial listed in Justia owner page 5109228): SaaS for measuring and tracking residential roofing. Our product's first vertical is roofing storm intelligence, so this is the closest business-context overlap I found.
- SCOUT SOFTWARE (Scout Software, LLC, 87805884); SCOUT CLOUD (Scout Cloud, Inc.); SCOUT (Software Robotics Corporation, 2019); several older registrations (e.g. 5484958, 5112452, 5570430, 4504571).
Whether "SCOUT OS" conflicts depends on the goods/services, the other marks' status and strength, and likelihood of confusion. That is a lawyer's call.

## "Ascend Software" collides with an existing company
- Ascend Software, LLC (owner page 5073471) holds ELEVATE ACCOUNTS PAYABLE (SaaS for accounts payable, Serial 97093039). Another ASCEND SOFTWARE, LLC (owner page 3992778) has SMARTTOUCH marks for business-automation software. Plus many ASCEND word marks in software.
- Risk: operating as "Ascend Software" in business-software SaaS may draw a conflict claim, and the business name in your entity filings may not protect you. **COUNSEL.**

## Recommendation (mine, not legal advice)
1. Do not spend on branding, domains-for-launch, or marketing under "Scout OS" or "Ascend Software" until counsel clears or you choose a different name. Naming is your decision (CLAUDE.md).
2. Shortlist 3–5 distinctive, coined candidates; I can screen each against public listings the same way before you pay counsel.
3. Keep the internal repo/package names; renaming the product is a text and config change, not an architecture change.
