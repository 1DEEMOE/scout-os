## "Ascend Software" collides with existing companies
- Ascend Software, LLC (owner page 5073471) holds ELEVATE ACCOUNTS PAYABLE (SaaS for accounts payable, Serial 97093039). Another ASCEND SOFTWARE, LLC (owner page 3992778) has SMARTTOUCH marks for business-automation software. Plus many ASCEND word marks in software.
- Risk: operating as "Ascend Software" in business-software SaaS may draw a conflict claim, and the business name in your entity filings may not protect you. **COUNSEL.**

## Recommendation (mine, not legal advice)
1. Do not spend on branding, launch domains, or marketing under "Ascend Software" until counsel clears it or you choose a different name or a distinguishing form (e.g. a coined product name under the Ascend umbrella). Naming is your decision (CLAUDE.md).
2. Shortlist 3–5 distinctive, coined candidates; I can screen each against public listings the same way before you pay counsel.
3. Keep the internal repo/package names; renaming the product is a text and config change, not an architecture change.
