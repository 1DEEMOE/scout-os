# Job Anatomy: Moving company

Status: DRAFT from operator practice and general knowledge of FMCSA rules. Regulatory items must be re-verified against current CFR text and state rules before launch (see `legalReview: pending`).

1. **Lifecycle:** inquiry → intake → survey (in-home, video, or inventory list) → estimate (binding/non-binding) → booking + deposit → prep (access, elevator, parking) → day-before reminder → load → transport → unload → delivery receipt/damage notes → invoice/balance → review → claims (if any) → referral/rebook.
2. **Intake:** local vs long, bedrooms, both addresses, date + flexibility, stairs/elevator/long carry, heavy items, packing needs, declared value + coverage choice, cubic feet (long distance).
3. **Pricing:** local = hourly by crew size + trip fee, minimum hours. Long distance = by weight/volume (this pack uses cubic feet). Add-ons: stairs, long carry, elevator, heavy items, packing labor/materials, valuation.
4. **Scheduling:** crew + truck resources, arrival windows, multi-day for long distance. Dispatch always human-approved.
5. **Documents:** estimate, inventory, order for service (interstate), bill of lading (interstate), valuation election, delivery receipt.
6. **Compliance (verify):** interstate household goods carriers: USDOT/MC authority; FMCSA consumer booklet before booking; estimate rules including delivery-collection limits on non-binding estimates; bill of lading; valuation options. Intrastate rules vary by state. Do not store card numbers, SSNs, gate/alarm codes.
7. **Revenue leaks:** missed calls, slow estimates, no deposit, unbilled stairs/long carry, no review ask.
8. **Signals:** new movers/home sales, seasonality (summer peak, month-end).
9. **KPIs:** quote win rate, speed to lead, average ticket, crew utilization, review rate.
10. **Integrations people use:** Google Calendar, Google Business Profile, QuickBooks, Stripe/Square (mover CRMs such as SmartMoving/MoverBase are common; not integrated here).
11. **AI map:** drafts: replies, estimate, reminders, review ask (tier 2). Needs tier 3: binding estimate, deposit request, invoice, claim reply. Manual: dispatch, damage assessment, claims decisions.
